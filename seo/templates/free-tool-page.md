# Template: free tool or download page

Use for downloadable templates and small calculators (cancellation policy, consent form, no-show cost calculator). They earn links and bring in owners who aren't ready to talk to us.

## Page

| Part | Guidance |
|---|---|
| H1 | "Free <thing> for spray tan studios" |
| Intro | Who it's for and what they get, in two sentences |
| The tool | A copy-paste block on the page (indexable text) first; a file download second |
| How to use it | 3 steps |
| Customize | What to change before using it (fees, notice window) |
| Disclaimer | "A starting point, not legal advice." |
| CTA | "Collect deposits and no-show fees automatically" to `/spray-tan-studio-software/`, plus Book a demo |
| FAQs | 3 to 4 |

## Rules

- No email gate for the first version. Don't collect addresses without a privacy review.
- Calculators: do the math in the page with no data sent anywhere. Show the formula in text.
- Use real numbers only if the user types them in. Don't show industry averages without a source.
- Page title: "<Thing> (Free <Template/Calculator>) | Happli".
- Add to `seo/keyword-map.csv` with intent "template" or "tool".
