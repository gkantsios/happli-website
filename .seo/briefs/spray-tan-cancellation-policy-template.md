## Angle

Give three ready-to-use policies (relaxed, standard, strict) that the reader can copy, with short reasons. Don't present any fee percentage as an industry standard unless it is sourced; present them as examples the owner should set. Happli lets owners set a deposit and a no-show fee as percentages of the service (see `/features/`), so the template maps to real settings.
