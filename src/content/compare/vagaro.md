*Last checked Oct 15, 2026. Vagaro details come from its [public pricing page](https://www.vagaro.com/pro/pricing). Prices and features change, so check their site before you decide.*

Vagaro is a general-purpose booking platform that includes a free Marketplace listing. Happli is booking software for spray tan and beauty studios, with an AI COO. Vagaro is broad and lower-priced. Happli is narrower and built around the spray tan day.

## At a glance

| | Happli | Vagaro |
|---|---|---|
| Starting price | Solo: $69/month ($59/month billed yearly) | $23.99/month, shown as an exclusive rate for the first 6 months |
| How it scales | Studio: $149/month per location ($129 billed yearly), up to 10 team members who take bookings, then $10/month each. Logins for everyone else are free | $10 (US) per bookable calendar license, up to seven per location; custom pricing for multiple locations |
| Free trial | Free until you go live, up to 30 days | 30 days, per its FAQ |
| Online booking | Yes, booking pages that plug into your existing website | Included in every plan, also on Instagram, Facebook and Apple Maps |

| Waitlist | Yes, with match alerts and gap offers you approve | Waitlist included |
| Client marketplace | No | Free Marketplace listing |
| Payment processing | Your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard rate, no Happli markup | Rates not stated on the pricing page |

## Pricing

Vagaro's pricing page lists **$23.99/month** for one location as an exclusive rate for the first 6 months. Each bookable calendar license is **$10 (US)**, up to seven licenses per business location; beyond seven, additional employees cost nothing extra. Multiple locations are custom-priced, with no added multi-location fee. The page says you can cancel any time with no cancellation fees, and lists a 30-day free trial in its FAQ.

Happli has two plans with the same features. **Solo** is $69/month ($59/month billed yearly) for one team member who takes bookings. **Studio** is $149/month per location ($129/month billed yearly) and includes up to 10 team members who take bookings, then $10/month each. Setup and client import are free, and Founding Studios get 25% off for as long as they stay subscribed. See the [pricing page](/pricing/) for details.

Vagaro is clearly the cheaper starting point. Happli costs more and aims to earn it back through fewer no-shows, fewer empty gaps and less time at the front desk.

## Where Vagaro is stronger

- **Lower price.**
- **A marketplace listing** and booking from Instagram, Facebook and Apple Maps, which can bring in new clients.
- **A very wide toolkit.** Loyalty programs, inventory, SOAP notes, forms, Daily Deals and more appear on its plan list.

## Where Happli is different

- **Built for spray tan studios.** Deposits, prep-instruction messages and mobile appointments with "on my way" texts are part of the product, not add-ons to a general tool.
- **A short list instead of a wide menu.** The Needs you list pulls open gaps, waitlist matches and drafted texts into one place, and gap offers go out only after you approve them.
- **Your money goes to you.** Charges are on your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard rate, with no Happli markup.
- **Free setup and client import**, from CSV.

## Who should pick Vagaro

Choose Vagaro if price is your top concern, if you want a marketplace to help new clients find you, or if you need a very broad set of tools (loyalty, inventory, payroll and more) in one place.

## Who should pick Happli

Choose Happli if you run a spray tan studio or beauty business and want a simpler product with hands-on setup and a daily short list of what needs you. [Book a demo](https://tidycal.com/gkantsios/30-minute-meeting) to see it with your own schedule.
