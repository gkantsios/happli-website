Vagaro is a general-purpose booking platform for salons, spas and other businesses, including tanning salons, with a free Marketplace listing.[^v-price][^v-tan] Happli is booking software for spray tan and beauty studios, with AI built in. Vagaro is broad and has the lower starting price. Happli is narrower and built around the spray tan day.

## Pricing

Vagaro's pricing page lists **$23.99/month** for one location as an exclusive rate for the first 6 months, with $30.00 shown struck through beside it. Each bookable calendar license is **$10 (US)**, up to seven licenses per business location; beyond seven, additional employees cost nothing extra. Multiple locations are custom-priced, with no added multi-location fee. The page says you can cancel any time with no cancellation fees, and its FAQ lists a 30-day free trial that excludes email marketing, text marketing and the branded app.[^v-price]

Some tools are paid add-ons on the same page: text marketing from $20/month, forms at $10/month, and payroll at $34/month plus $5 per employee. Email marketing is free to start, with 1,000 free emails a month included.[^v-price]

Happli has one plan with every feature. At launch pricing it's $52/month ($44/month billed yearly) for one team member who takes bookings, plus $10/month for each extra one. Each plan includes 1 location, and extra locations are half price. Front desk and admin logins are free, and your website is included. Setup and client import are free, there are no contracts, and you keep launch pricing for as long as you stay subscribed. See the [pricing page](/pricing/) for details.[^happli]

Vagaro is clearly the cheaper starting point. Happli costs more and aims to earn it back through fewer no-shows, fewer empty gaps and less time at the front desk.

## Where Vagaro is stronger

- **Lower starting price.**[^v-price]
- **A marketplace listing** and booking from Instagram, Facebook and Apple Maps, which can bring in new clients.[^v-price]
- **A very wide toolkit.** Loyalty programs, inventory, SOAP notes, Daily Deals and AI marketing tools are on its included list, and forms and payroll are available as paid add-ons.[^v-price]

## Where Happli is different

- **Built for spray tan studios.** Deposits, prep-instruction messages and mobile appointments with "on my way" texts are part of the product, set up for a spray tan day.[^happli]
- **A short list instead of a wide menu.** The Needs you list pulls open gaps, waitlist matches and drafted texts into one place, and gap offers go out only after you approve them.[^happli]
- **Your money goes to you.** Charges are on your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard rate, with no Happli markup.[^happli]
- **Free setup and client import**, from CSV, done by our team.[^happli]

## Who should pick Vagaro

Choose Vagaro if price is your top concern, if you want a marketplace to help new clients find you, or if you need a very broad set of tools (loyalty, inventory and more, with payroll as a paid add-on) in one place.[^v-price]
