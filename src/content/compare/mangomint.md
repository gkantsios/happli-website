*Last checked Oct 15, 2026. Mangomint details come from its [public pricing page](https://www.mangomint.com/pricing/). Prices and features change, so check their site before you decide.*

Mangomint is a booking and payments system with a pricing model built on locations and users. Happli is booking software for spray tan and beauty studios, with AI built in and a short list of what needs you. Both handle online booking, reminders and payments. They differ in price, how they charge, and how much of the day they try to run for you.

## Pricing

Mangomint's pricing page shows no named plans. You build a price from a per-location fee and a per-user fee: **$120/month per location** and **$10/month per user**, where anyone with a profile counts as a user. Optional add-ons include a phone line ($70/month per line), marketing (from $30/month) and payroll ($50/month plus $8 per worker). The page lists free onboarding and free data transfer.

Happli has two plans with the same features. **Solo** is $69/month ($59/month billed yearly) for one team member who takes bookings. **Studio** is $149/month per location ($129/month billed yearly) and includes up to 10 team members who take bookings, then $10/month each. Front desk and admin logins are free on both. An optional Marketing add-on is $39/month. Founding Studios get 25% off the plans for as long as they stay subscribed. See the [pricing page](/pricing/) for details.

For a solo artist with one location, Happli Solo costs less than Mangomint's $120 location fee plus $10 for the user. For a team at one location, Happli Studio covers up to 10 team members who take bookings for $149, and logins for everyone else are free; with Mangomint, add $10 for each user to the $120 location fee.

## Where Mangomint is stronger

- **A wider add-on list.** Its pricing page lists payroll and a phone add-on, which Happli doesn't offer.
- **Built-in payment processing** with published rates, if you want one vendor for software and payments.

## Where Happli is different

- **Spray tan is the niche, not an afterthought.** Prep-instruction messages, mobile appointments with "on my way" texts, and deposits are set up with studios like yours in mind.
- **The Needs you list.** Open gaps, waitlist matches and drafted texts collect in one place. For a gap, you pick one client and Happli drafts the text. It sends only after you approve it.
- **Payments go straight to you.** Charges are made on your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard rate, with no Happli markup.
- **You pay for team members who take bookings, not logins.** Front desk and admin logins are always free.

## Who should pick Mangomint

Choose Mangomint if you run a larger salon or spa and want payroll and a phone system in the same product, or if you want software and payment processing from one vendor.
