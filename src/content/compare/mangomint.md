*Last checked Oct 15, 2026. Mangomint details come from its [public pricing page](https://www.mangomint.com/pricing/). Prices and features change, so check their site before you decide.*

Mangomint is a booking and payments system with a pricing model built on locations and users. Happli is booking software for spray tan and beauty studios, with an AI COO that keeps a short list of what needs you. Both handle online booking, reminders and payments. They differ in price, how they charge, and how much of the day they try to run for you.

## Pricing

Mangomint's pricing page shows no named plans. You build a price from a per-location fee and a per-user fee: **$120/month per location** and **$10/month per user**, where anyone with a profile counts as a user. Optional add-ons include a phone line ($70/month per line), marketing (from $30/month) and payroll ($50/month plus $8 per worker). The page lists free onboarding and free data transfer.

Happli is priced by location only: **$99/month for 1 location, $149 for 2, and $199 for 3**, as founding member pricing. Normal email and SMS usage is included during the founding phase; see the [pricing page](/pricing/) for the fine print.

For a solo artist with one location, Happli's price is lower than Mangomint's $120 location fee plus $10 for the user. For a larger team at one location, add up Mangomint's per-user charges against Happli's flat location price.

## Where Mangomint is stronger

- **A wider add-on list.** Its pricing page lists payroll, a phone add-on and tiered marketing credits, which Happli doesn't offer.
- **Memberships** are listed as a core feature.
- **A free trial** is offered.
- **Built-in payment processing** with published rates, if you want one vendor for software and payments.

## Where Happli is different

- **Spray tan is the niche, not an afterthought.** Prep-instruction messages, mobile appointments with "on my way" texts, and deposits are set up with studios like yours in mind.
- **The Needs you list.** Open gaps, waitlist matches and drafted texts collect in one place. For a gap, you pick one client and Happli drafts the text. It sends only after you approve it.
- **Payments go straight to you.** Charges are made on your own Stripe account, and Happli takes no fee on them.
- **Flat, per-location pricing** instead of per-user charges.

## Who should pick Mangomint

Choose Mangomint if you run a larger salon or spa and want payroll, marketing credits and a phone system in the same product, if you want memberships today, or if you want to try the software free before paying.

## Who should pick Happli

Choose Happli if you are a spray tan studio or beauty pro who wants online booking, deposits and reminders without per-user fees, and a short list of the few things each day that need you.
