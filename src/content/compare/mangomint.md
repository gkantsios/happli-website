Mangomint is a booking and payments system with a pricing model built on locations and users.[^m-price] Happli is booking software for spray tan and beauty studios, with AI built in and a short list of what needs you. Both handle online booking, reminders, payments and a waitlist.[^m-price][^m-sched] They differ in price, how they charge, and how much of the day they try to run for you.

## Pricing

Mangomint's pricing page shows no named plans. You build a price from a per-location fee and a per-user fee: **$120/month per location** and **$10/month per user**, where anyone with a profile counts as a user. Optional add-ons include a phone line ($70/month per line), marketing (from $30/month) and payroll ($50/month plus $8 per worker). The page lists free onboarding and free data transfer.[^m-price]

Happli has one plan with every feature. At launch pricing it's $52/month ($44/month billed yearly) for one team member who takes bookings, plus $10/month for each extra one. Each plan includes 1 location, and extra locations are half price. Front desk and admin logins are free, and your website is included. Setup and client import are free, there are no contracts, and launch pricing is locked for the features in your plan for as long as you stay subscribed. New features added later may cost more. See the [pricing page](/pricing/) for details.[^happli]

For a solo artist with one location, Happli's $52 launch price costs less than Mangomint's $120 location fee plus $10 for the user. For a team at one location, Happli adds $10/month for each extra team member who takes bookings, and logins for everyone else are free; with Mangomint, add $10 for each user to the $120 location fee.[^m-price]

## Where Mangomint is stronger

- **A wider add-on list.** Its pricing page lists payroll and a phone add-on, which Happli doesn't offer.[^m-price]
- **Built-in payment processing** with published rates, if you want one vendor for software and payments.[^m-price]

## Where Happli is different

- **Spray tan is the niche, not an afterthought.** Prep-instruction messages, mobile appointments with "on my way" texts, and deposits are set up with studios like yours in mind.[^happli]
- **The Needs you list.** Both products match openings with a waitlist. In Happli, open gaps, waitlist matches and drafted texts collect in one place: for a gap, you pick one client, Happli drafts the text, and it sends only after you approve it.[^happli][^m-sched]
- **Payments go straight to you.** Charges are made on your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard rate, with no Happli markup.[^happli]
- **You pay for team members who take bookings, not logins.** Front desk and admin logins are always free.[^happli]

## Who should pick Mangomint

Choose Mangomint if you run a larger salon or spa and want payroll and a phone system in the same product, or if you want software and payment processing from one vendor.[^m-price]

## Who should pick Happli

Choose Happli if you are a spray tan studio or beauty pro who wants online booking, deposits and reminders without paying for every login, and a short list of the few things each day that need you.
