MySunless is software made for spray tan professionals, and it's the closest like-for-like option to Happli. Both are built around spray tan and sunless studios. MySunless is the lower-priced choice. Happli adds a short daily list of what needs you and hands-on setup.

## Pricing

MySunless lists two plans. **Starter** is $20/month for one team member and 100 clients. **Pro** is $45/month for five team members and unlimited clients. Both include unlimited SMS, online booking with a branded booking site, client notes, intake forms and photos, memberships and packages, a Stripe and cash POS, and reports. Annual billing saves up to 25%, and every plan has a 14-day free trial.

Happli has two plans with the same features. **Solo** is $69/month ($59/month billed yearly) for one team member who takes bookings. **Studio** is $149/month per location ($129/month billed yearly) and includes up to 10 team members who take bookings, then $10/month each. Happli is free until your studio goes live, for up to 30 days, and Founding Studios get 25% off for as long as they stay subscribed. See the [pricing page](/pricing/) for details.

MySunless costs far less, and its Starter and Pro plans are a strong fit for solo artists. Happli is the higher-priced option, so the question is whether its extras are worth the difference for your studio.

## Where MySunless is stronger

- **Price.** $20 or $45 a month, with a free 14-day trial.
- **Unlimited SMS** on every plan.
- **Spray-tan-only focus**, with the whole product aimed at sunless professionals.

## Where Happli is different

- **The Needs you list and gap offers.** Open gaps and waitlist matches collect in one list, and Happli drafts a text offering the time to a client you pick. It sends only after you approve it.
- **Free setup and client import**, done with you, and free until you go live.
- **Multiple locations** on one account, with a booking page for each.
- **Reports** covering money collected, tips, deposits, no-show fees, package sales, refunds, and results by team member, service and location, with CSV export.

## Who should pick MySunless

Choose MySunless if you're a solo or small-team spray tan artist who wants the lowest price and unlimited texting, and you're comfortable setting things up yourself.

## Who should pick Happli

Choose Happli if you run a studio (or more than one), want a short daily list of gaps and drafts that need you, and prefer to have your setup and import handled for you. [Book a demo](https://tidycal.com/gkantsios/30-minute-meeting) to see it with your own schedule.
