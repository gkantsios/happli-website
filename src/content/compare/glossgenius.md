GlossGenius is all-in-one booking, payments and business management software for beauty and wellness businesses, including tanning studios.[^g-home] Happli is booking software for spray tan and beauty studios, with AI built in. Both give you online booking and client messaging. The biggest differences are how plans are tiered and what you get at each level.

## Pricing

GlossGenius charges by tier. **Standard** is $28/month billed monthly, or $24/month billed annually. **Gold** is $56/month ($48 annually) and is aimed at businesses with up to 9 staff. **Platinum** is $168/month ($148 annually) for established businesses and teams of 10 or more. Additional locations are charged the plan price at a 15% discount. Deposits and cancellation and no-show policies are on every tier; text marketing includes 500 text credits on Gold and 2,500 on Platinum.[^g-price]

Happli has two plans with the same features. **Solo** is $69/month ($59/month billed yearly) for one team member who takes bookings. **Studio** is $149/month ($129/month billed yearly) and includes up to 10 team members who take bookings at each location, then $10/month each. Each plan includes 1 location, and extra locations are half the plan price. Front desk and admin logins are free on both. Setup and client import are free, and Founding Studios get 25% off for as long as they stay subscribed. See the [pricing page](/pricing/) for details.[^happli]

On sticker price, GlossGenius's Standard plan is cheaper than Happli Solo. The right comparison is what you need: GlossGenius puts forms and waivers, the automated waitlist and Google booking on the Gold tier, so a studio that wants those will compare Gold ($56/month) with Happli.[^g-price]

## Where GlossGenius is stronger

- **A lower entry price** and a clear ladder of tiers as you grow.[^g-price]
- **A 14-day free trial** with no credit card.[^g-price]
- **A broad marketing toolkit.** Email and text marketing, inventory and retail management, and Google booking and reviews appear in its plan lists, along with AI tools such as an AI marketing assistant.[^g-price]
- **Payouts.** Free same-day payouts Monday through Thursday, with instant payouts for an extra 1.8%.[^g-price]

## Where Happli is different

- **Spray tan first.** Deposits, prep-instruction messages and mobile appointments with "on my way" texts are part of how Happli works.[^happli]
- **The Needs you list and gap offers.** Open gaps and waitlist matches collect in one list. Happli drafts a text offering the time to the client you choose, and sends it only after you approve it.[^happli]
- **Money goes straight to you.** Charges are made on your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard rate, with no Happli markup.[^happli]
- **Every feature on both plans.** Nothing is locked behind a higher tier; you pay for the team members who take bookings.[^happli]

## Who should pick GlossGenius

Choose GlossGenius if you're just starting out and want the lowest monthly price, or want one platform for booking, marketing and retail.[^g-price]
