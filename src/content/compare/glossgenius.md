*Last checked Oct 15, 2026. GlossGenius details come from its [public pricing page](https://glossgenius.com/pricing). Prices and features change, so check their site before you decide.*

GlossGenius is an all-in-one booking, payments and marketing platform for beauty and wellness businesses. Happli is booking software for spray tan and beauty studios, with an AI COO. Both give you an online booking site and client messaging. The biggest differences are how plans are tiered and what you get at each level.

## At a glance

| | Happli | GlossGenius |
|---|---|---|
| Starting price | $99/month for 1 location (founding member pricing) | Standard: $28/month billed monthly ($24/month billed annually) |
| Plan structure | By number of locations: $99, $149, $199 | By feature tier: Standard, Gold ($56/month, up to 9 staff), Platinum ($168/month) |
| Free trial | None listed. Book a call to see it first | 14 days, no credit card required, per its FAQ |
| Online booking | Yes, a booking page for each location | Yes, 24/7 online booking and a custom booking website |
| Reminders | Yes, text, email or both, with your own wording | Unlimited client notifications on every tier |
| Packages | Yes, prepaid session packages | Packages and memberships from Standard |
| Memberships | Not listed on our features page | Included from Standard |
| Waitlist | Yes, with match alerts and gap offers you approve | Automated waitlist on Gold and above |
| Forms and waivers | Not listed on our features page | Gold and above |
| Payment processing | Your own Stripe account, no Happli fee | Processing rates not shown in the text of its pricing page; instant payouts cost an extra 1.8% |

## Pricing

GlossGenius charges by tier. **Standard** is $28/month billed monthly, or $24/month billed annually. **Gold** is $56/month ($48 annually) and is aimed at businesses with up to 9 staff. **Platinum** is $168/month ($148 annually) for established businesses and teams of 10 or more. Additional locations are charged the plan price at a 15% discount.

Happli is priced by location: **$99/month for 1 location, $149 for 2, and $199 for 3**, as founding member pricing.

On sticker price, GlossGenius's Standard plan is cheaper than Happli's single-location plan. The right comparison is what you need: GlossGenius puts forms and waivers, the automated waitlist and Google booking on the Gold tier, so a studio that wants those will compare Gold ($56/month) with Happli.

## Where GlossGenius is stronger

- **A lower entry price** and a clear ladder of tiers as you grow.
- **A 14-day free trial** with no credit card.
- **Memberships** and **forms and waivers** are listed on its plans.
- **A broad marketing toolkit.** Email and text marketing, inventory and retail management, and Google booking and reviews appear in its plan lists.
- **Payouts.** Free same-day payouts Monday through Thursday, with instant payouts for an extra 1.8%.

## Where Happli is different

- **Spray tan first.** Deposits, prep-instruction messages and mobile appointments with "on my way" texts are part of how Happli works.
- **The Needs you list and gap offers.** Open gaps and waitlist matches collect in one list. Happli drafts a text offering the time to the client you choose, and sends it only after you approve it.
- **Money goes straight to you.** Charges are made on your own Stripe account with no Happli fee.
- **Pricing by location, not by feature tier.** Every plan has the same features; you pay for the number of locations.

## Who should pick GlossGenius

Choose GlossGenius if you're just starting out and want the lowest monthly price, want a free trial, need memberships or forms and waivers now, or want one platform for booking, marketing and retail.

## Who should pick Happli

Choose Happli if you are a spray tan studio or beauty pro who wants booking, deposits and reminders plus a short daily list of what needs you, and you'd rather pay by location than climb feature tiers. [Book a demo](https://tidycal.com/gkantsios/30-minute-meeting) to see it with your own schedule.
