## Our take

**Square Appointments is the better fit for a solo artist or a small studio that wants to start at $0 a month,** see its card rates in advance, and add staff without paying per calendar. Its Free plan includes online booking, reminders and unlimited staff calendars.[^s-price] **Vagaro is the better fit for a studio that wants a broad toolkit in one subscription**: memberships, packages, loyalty, a waitlist, resource scheduling and a free Marketplace listing, at $23.99/month for the first 6 months, plus $10 for each bookable calendar you add.[^v-price]

The caveats cut both ways. Square's no-show fees, waitlist and multi-staff booking start on Plus at $49/month per location, and resource management needs Premium at $149.[^s-price] Vagaro doesn't publish its card-processing rates on its pricing page, so get a quote before you sign.[^v-price] And neither company's help article describes a setting that requires approval for some services but not others.[^v-rules][^s-settings]

## Pricing

**Vagaro** lists one subscription at **$23.99/month** for one location as an exclusive rate for the first 6 months, with $30.00 shown struck through beside it. Each bookable calendar license, meaning someone who performs services rather than front desk or admin staff, costs **$10 (US)**, up to seven per location. Each extra location has its own cost, with no multi-location fee on top. You can cancel any time with no cancellation fee, though the free card reader requires 12 months of processing with Vagaro Merchant Services, or a $150 early cancellation fee.[^v-price]

Vagaro's add-ons are priced separately on the same page: text marketing from $20/month, forms $10/month, the Online Shopping Cart (which also handles deposits) $10/month, Vagaro MySite $20/month, a branded app $100/month and payroll $34/month plus $5 per employee, among others. Email marketing is free to start, with 1,000 free emails a month included.[^v-price]

**Square Appointments** has three published plans, each priced per location:[^s-price]

Square also lists custom pricing for businesses that process over $250,000 a year.[^s-price]

For a solo artist, Square Free costs nothing until you take a payment, and Vagaro starts at $23.99 a month. For a team, the math depends on headcount: Vagaro adds $10 per bookable calendar, while Square charges nothing per person but moves you to $49 a month as soon as you want no-show fees or a waitlist.

## Booking approval, in detail

This is where the two differ most quietly. **Vagaro** lets you require acceptance for online bookings with two filters: which employees it applies to, and which types of customers it applies to. One customer type is "a customer with no-shows or cancellations," where you choose how many before approval kicks in.[^v-rules] That's useful if you want to vet a new team member's bookings or clients with a history of missed appointments.

**Square** has a single Reservation Guarantee setting. Your business either automatically accepts all appointment requests, or must manually accept or decline all of them. With manual acceptance on, requests show as pending until you accept or decline them, and clients can cancel while a request is still pending.[^s-settings][^s-accept]

Neither help article describes requiring approval for some services and not others, for example a consultation-only treatment that needs a quick check while routine appointments book instantly.

## Pick Vagaro if…

- You want memberships, packages, loyalty, a waitlist and room or equipment scheduling in the base subscription, without moving to a higher tier.[^v-price]
- You want new clients to find you through Apple Maps as well as a marketplace. Vagaro lists both; Square's marketplace is Square Go.[^v-price][^s-go]
- You want approval only for certain team members, or only for clients with a history of no-shows or cancellations.[^v-rules]
- You have seven or fewer people who take bookings and are fine with $10 per calendar, or more than seven and want the extra people at no added cost.[^v-price]

## Pick Square if…

- You want to pay $0/month to start and see your card-processing rates before you sign up.[^s-price]
- You want deposits or prepayments on a free plan.[^s-price]
- You want to add as many staff calendars as you like without a per-person charge.[^s-price]
- You want one simple approval switch: every online request confirms instantly, or every one waits for you.[^s-settings]
