Vagaro starts low and adds $10 for each extra bookable calendar, with forms, a website and text marketing as paid add-ons. Happli has one plan with those in it. Vagaro costs less at every team size, even after its intro rate ends.

## Pricing

Vagaro's pricing page lists **$23.99 a month as an intro rate for the first 6 months**, then **$30**, plus **$10 a month for each extra bookable calendar**, up to 7. Forms are a $10 add-on, a website $20, and text marketing starts at $20.[^v]

If you'd add forms and a website, one person on Vagaro is $23.99 + $10 + $20 = **$53.99 a month** during the intro rate and **$60** after it, against Happli's $52.[^v][^happli]

Happli has one plan with everything in it. At launch pricing it's $52 a month ($44 a month billed yearly) for one person who takes bookings, plus $10 a month for each extra one. Front desk and admin logins are free, your website is included, setup and client import are free, and there are no contracts.[^happli] Card payments go to your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢, and Happli takes no cut.[^stripe]

## Where Vagaro is stronger

- **A lower base price.** $23.99 during the intro rate and $30 after it, against Happli's $52.[^v][^happli]
- **Lower at every team size.** The lowest price in our chart for a team of 3 while the intro rate lasts, and still below Happli after it ends.[^v]
- **Pay only for what you add.** If you don't need forms, a website or text marketing, you don't pay for them.[^v]

## Where Happli is different

- **A website built for you.** Our team at gohappybeauty builds it, it stays in sync with Happli, and it's included in the plan.[^happli]
- **The Needs you list.** What needs a decision today sits in one short list, and nothing drafted by AI goes to a client until you approve it.[^happli]
- **A day book built for your phone**, so you can run the day between appointments.[^happli]

- **Free setup by a real person**, and we import your clients from a CSV export.[^happli]

Choose Vagaro if the lowest monthly price matters most, or if you'd rather pay for add-ons one at a time than have them in the plan.[^v]
