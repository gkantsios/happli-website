*Every GlossGenius claim below links to GlossGenius's own pricing page, checked Nov 21, 2026. Prices and plans change, so check their site before you decide.*

GlossGenius has three tiers, and its Gold plan covers up to 9 staff for one flat price. Happli has one plan priced by the people who take bookings. GlossGenius costs less at every team size in our chart.

## At a glance

| | Happli | GlossGenius |
|---|---|---|
| Plans | One plan: $52/month launch price ($44/month billed yearly)[^happli] | Standard $28/month ($24 billed yearly), Gold $56 ($48), Platinum $168[^gg] |
| How it scales | +$10/month for each extra person who takes bookings; front desk and admin logins are free[^happli] | Gold covers up to 9 staff[^gg] |
| Solo / team of 3 / team of 5 | $52 / $72 / $92 a month[^happli] | Standard $28; Gold $56 / $56 a month[^gg] |
| Card rates | Your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢; Happli takes no cut[^stripe] | 2.4% + $0.15 in person; 3.4% + $0.15 card on file or online[^gg] |
| Forms | Forms and waivers included, with AI form import you review[^happli] | Forms and waitlist start on Gold[^gg] |

The monthly totals use the rules on the [compare page](/compare-v2/): bookable people only, one location, billed monthly in US dollars, software only. Solo uses Standard; teams use Gold.

## Pricing

GlossGenius lists **Standard at $28 a month** ($24 billed yearly), **Gold at $56** ($48 billed yearly) for up to 9 staff, and **Platinum at $168**. Its card rates are 2.4% + $0.15 in person and 3.4% + $0.15 for card on file or online.[^gg]

Happli has one plan with everything in it. At launch pricing it's $52 a month ($44 a month billed yearly) for one person who takes bookings, plus $10 a month for each extra one. Front desk and admin logins are free, your website is included, setup and client import are free, and there are no contracts.[^happli] Card payments go to your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢, and Happli takes no cut.[^stripe]

## Where GlossGenius is stronger

- **A lower price at every size.** $28 for one person and $56 for a team on Gold, against Happli's $52, $72 and $92.[^gg][^happli]
- **One flat price for up to 9 staff** on Gold, with forms and waitlist included.[^gg]
- **A lower in-person card rate.** 2.4% + $0.15, against Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢.[^gg][^stripe]

## Where Happli is different

- **The Needs you list.** What needs a decision today sits in one short list, and nothing drafted by AI goes to a client until you approve it.[^happli]
- **A day book built for your phone**, so you can run the day between appointments.[^happli]
- **Forms on every plan.** Forms and waivers are included, and AI imports your existing form for you to review.[^happli]
- **Your money goes to you.** Online and card-on-file charges land in your own Stripe account at 2.9% + 30¢, and Happli takes no cut.[^stripe]
- **Free setup by a real person**, and we import your clients from a CSV export.[^happli]

Choose GlossGenius if price matters most, if you have a team of up to 9 and want one flat bill, or if most of your payments are taken in person.[^gg]
