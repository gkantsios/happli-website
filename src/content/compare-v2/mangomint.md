*Every Mangomint claim below links to Mangomint's own pricing page, checked Nov 21, 2026. Prices and plans change, so check their site before you decide.*

Mangomint charges a price per location plus a price per user. Happli charges by the people who take bookings, with front desk and admin logins free. Here Happli costs less at every team size, and Mangomint has the lower in-person card rate.

## At a glance

| | Happli | Mangomint |
|---|---|---|
| Base price | $52/month launch price ($44/month billed yearly)[^happli] | $120/month per location[^mm] |
| How it scales | +$10/month for each extra person who takes bookings; front desk and admin logins are free[^happli] | +$10/month per user[^mm] |
| Solo / team of 3 / team of 5 | $52 / $72 / $92 a month[^happli] | $130 / $150 / $170 a month[^mm] |
| Card rates | Your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢; Happli takes no cut[^stripe] | 2.45% + 15¢ in person; 2.90% + 30¢ virtual[^mm] |
| Website | Built for you by our team at gohappybeauty, kept in sync with Happli, and included[^happli] | No website or website builder listed; online booking is an overlay or link you add to a site you already have[^mm-booking][^mm-site] |
| Deposits and cards on file | Deposits on your own Stripe account[^happli] | Need Mangomint's own payments[^mm] |

The monthly totals use the rules on the [compare page](/compare-v2/): bookable people only, one location, billed monthly in US dollars, software only. We count each bookable person as one Mangomint user ($120 + 3 × $10 = $150 for a team of 3).

## Pricing

Mangomint lists **$120 a month per location** plus **$10 a month per user**. Its card rates are 2.45% + 15¢ in person and 2.90% + 30¢ for virtual payments, and deposits and cards on file need Mangomint's own payments.[^mm]

Happli has one plan with everything in it. At launch pricing it's $52 a month ($44 a month billed yearly) for one person who takes bookings, plus $10 a month for each extra one. Front desk and admin logins are free, your website is included, setup and client import are free, and there are no contracts.[^happli] Card payments go to your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢, and Happli takes no cut.[^stripe]

## Where Mangomint is stronger

- **A lower in-person card rate.** 2.45% + 15¢, against Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢. Its virtual rate, 2.90% + 30¢, matches Stripe's.[^mm][^stripe]
- **Payments in one system.** Deposits and cards on file run through Mangomint's own payments.[^mm]

## Where Happli is different

- **A website built for you.** Our team at gohappybeauty builds it, it stays in sync with Happli, and it's included in the plan.[^happli]
- **A lower monthly price.** $52, $72 and $92 for one, three and five people, against $130, $150 and $170.[^happli][^mm]
- **The Needs you list.** What needs a decision today sits in one short list, and nothing drafted by AI goes to a client until you approve it.[^happli]
- **A day book built for your phone**, so you can run the day between appointments.[^happli]
- **Your own Stripe account.** Deposits, checkout, tips and gift cards land in your own Stripe account, and Happli takes no cut. If you leave, your payments account stays yours.[^stripe]
- **Free setup by a real person**, and we import your clients from a CSV export.[^happli]

## Who should pick Mangomint

Choose Mangomint if most of your payments are taken in person and the lower in-person card rate matters to you, or if you'd rather have payments and booking in one system.[^mm]
