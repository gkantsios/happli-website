*Every Fresha claim below links to Fresha's own pricing page, checked Nov 21, 2026. Prices and plans change, so check their site before you decide.*

Fresha has the cheapest plan in our chart for one person, a price per team member for teams, and a marketplace that can send you new clients for a one-time fee. Happli has one plan priced by the people who take bookings. Fresha costs less at every team size.

## At a glance

| | Happli | Fresha |
|---|---|---|
| Plans | One plan: $52/month launch price ($44/month billed yearly)[^happli] | Independent $19.95/month; Team $14.95/month per bookable member[^fr] |
| How it scales | +$10/month for each extra person who takes bookings; front desk and admin logins are free[^happli] | $14.95/month for each bookable team member[^fr] |
| Solo / team of 3 / team of 5 | $52 / $72 / $92 a month[^happli] | $19.95 / $44.85 / $74.75 a month[^fr] |
| Website | Built for you by our team at gohappybeauty, kept in sync with Happli, and included[^happli] | Direct booking links are free; a professional site is the Smart Website add-on, $15.95/month, a builder that updates from your Fresha account. Not built for you[^fr][^fr-site] |
| New clients from a marketplace | No marketplace | 20% one-time fee per new client, $6 minimum[^fr] |
| Card rates | Your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢; Happli takes no cut[^stripe] | 2.79% + $0.20 online; 2.29% + $0.20 in person[^fr] |

The monthly totals use the rules on the [compare page](/compare-v2/): bookable people only, one location, billed monthly in US dollars, software only. Solo uses Independent; teams use Team ($14.95 × 3 = $44.85, × 5 = $74.75).

## Pricing

Fresha lists **Independent at $19.95 a month** and **Team at $14.95 a month per bookable member**. New clients who come through its marketplace cost a **20% one-time fee** ($6 minimum). Card rates are 2.79% + $0.20 online and 2.29% + $0.20 in person.[^fr]

Happli has one plan with everything in it. At launch pricing it's $52 a month ($44 a month billed yearly) for one person who takes bookings, plus $10 a month for each extra one. Front desk and admin logins are free, your website is included, setup and client import are free, and there are no contracts.[^happli] Card payments go to your own Stripe account at Stripe's standard 2.9% + 30¢, and Happli takes no cut.[^stripe]

## Where Fresha is stronger

- **The lowest price for one person** in our chart, $19.95 a month, and lower than Happli at every team size.[^fr][^happli]
- **A marketplace** that can send you new clients, for a one-time fee only when it does.[^fr]
- **Lower card rates.** 2.79% + $0.20 online and 2.29% + $0.20 in person, against Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢.[^fr][^stripe]

## Where Happli is different

- **A website built for you.** Our team at gohappybeauty builds it, it stays in sync with Happli, and it's included in the plan.[^happli]
- **The Needs you list.** What needs a decision today sits in one short list, and nothing drafted by AI goes to a client until you approve it.[^happli]
- **A day book built for your phone**, so you can run the day between appointments.[^happli]
- **One plan, nothing to add on.** Deposits, checkout, tips, no-show links, gift cards and packages; reminders in your own wording; forms and waivers, with AI that imports your existing form for you to review; review-request texts; and a website.[^happli]

- **Free setup by a real person**, and we import your clients from a CSV export.[^happli]

## Who should pick Fresha

Choose Fresha if you work on your own and want the lowest monthly price, or if you want a marketplace to help new clients find you and are happy to pay a one-time fee when it does.[^fr]
