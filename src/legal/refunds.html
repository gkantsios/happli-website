Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy covers your Happli subscription. It doesn't cover payments your clients make to your studio; your studio sets its own refund, deposit and cancellation policies for clients, and refunds to clients are issued by your studio through Stripe.

Free until you go live

New studios can use Happli free until they go live, for up to 30 days. If you cancel before then, you won't be charged. [CONFIRM: what counts as "going live"]

Cancelling

  • You can cancel anytime from [CONFIRM: where in the app, or by emailing hello@gohappli.com].
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current monthly or yearly billing period. You keep access until then and won't be charged again.
  • Cancelling the Marketing add-on works the same way and doesn't affect your plan.
  • If you're on Founding Studio pricing, cancelling ends it. If you come back later, you'll pay the normal price.

Refunds

  • Subscription fees are non-refundable, including for partial months or unused time on a yearly plan, except where the law requires or we agree otherwise in writing. [CONFIRM: any refund window for yearly plans]
  • If we charge you by mistake, contact us and we'll refund the error.
  • Extra marketing text charges are for texts already sent and aren't refundable.

Plan changes

You can move between Solo and Studio, or between monthly and yearly. [CONFIRM: proration, and whether downgrades take effect immediately or at renewal] Founding Studio pricing carries over when you move from Solo to Studio.

Your data after cancelling

You can request an export of your data after cancelling, as described in our Terms of Service. After that, it's deleted as described in our Privacy Policy.
