Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy covers your Happli subscription. It doesn't cover payments your clients make to your studio; your studio sets its own refund, deposit and cancellation policies for clients, and refunds to clients are issued by your studio through Stripe.

Free until you go live

New studios can use Happli free until they go live, for up to 30 days. A card on file is required to start. "Going live" means your studio starts taking real client bookings or payments in Happli, and the free period ends when you go live or after 30 days, whichever comes first. If you cancel before then, you won't be charged.

Canceling

  • You can cancel anytime by emailing hello@gohappli.com. There's no long-term contract.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current monthly or yearly billing period. You keep access until then and won't be charged again.
  • Canceling the Marketing add-on works the same way and doesn't affect your plan.
  • Unused Marketing credits don't roll over to the next month.
  • If you're on launch pricing, canceling ends it. If you come back later, you'll pay the price in effect then.
  • Launch pricing and its price protection don't apply to the Marketing add-on or extra credits. Add-on prices may change with at least 30 days' notice.

Refunds

  • There are no automatic refunds, including for partial months or unused time on a yearly plan. We review refund requests case by case; email us and we'll take a look. Refunds are also given where the law requires.
  • If we charge you by mistake, contact us and we'll refund the error.
  • Extra credit charges are for credits already used and aren't refundable.

Plan changes

You can add or remove team members who take bookings and locations, or move between monthly and yearly. Additions take effect right away and are prorated. Reductions take effect at your next renewal. Launch pricing carries over when you add team members or locations. It covers the features in your plan; new features added later may cost more.

Your data after canceling

You can request an export of your data after canceling, as described in our Terms of Service. After that, it's deleted as described in our Privacy Policy.

Contact
