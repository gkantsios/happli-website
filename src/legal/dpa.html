Data Processing Addendum

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is part of the Terms of Service between Happli (Happy Industries, Inc. dba Happli) and the studio using the Service ("Studio"). It applies to personal information about the Studio's clients that Happli processes on the Studio's behalf ("Client Data"). If the DPA and the Terms conflict about Client Data, the DPA wins.

1. Roles

The Studio is the controller (business) and Happli is the processor (service provider) of Client Data. The Studio is responsible for having a lawful basis and any required consent for collecting Client Data and for messages sent to its clients.

2. Instructions

Happli processes Client Data only to provide the Service, as the Studio directs through its settings and use of the Service, and as required by law. Happli will tell the Studio if it believes an instruction breaks the law.

3. Service provider commitments

Happli will not sell or share Client Data; will not keep, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship with the Studio or for any purpose other than providing the Service; and will not combine it with personal information from other sources except as the law allows. Happli will comply with applicable obligations under the CCPA/CPRA and give the same level of protection it requires.

4. Confidentiality and security

Anyone at Happli with access to Client Data is bound by confidentiality. Happli maintains reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit, per-studio isolation enforced by database row-level security, passwordless sign-in, role-based access, and an append-only audit log. Client first names only are used in AI prompts; phone numbers and email addresses are never included.

5. Subprocessors

The Studio authorizes Happli to use the subprocessors listed in the Privacy Policy. Happli will bind each to data protection terms at least as protective as this DPA and remains responsible for them. Happli will update the list and email the Studio at least 14 days before adding a subprocessor that handles Client Data. The Studio may object on reasonable data protection grounds. If Happli can't resolve the objection, the Studio may cancel before the new subprocessor handles its Client Data, and Happli will refund the unused part of any prepaid subscription fees.

6. Assistance and requests

Happli will pass to the Studio any request it receives from a Studio client and will reasonably help the Studio respond to requests to access, correct or delete Client Data.

7. Security incidents

Happli will notify the Studio of a security incident affecting the Studio's Client Data without undue delay after discovering it, and in any case within 72 hours of confirming it, with the information reasonably available to help the Studio meet its own notice obligations.

8. Return and deletion

When the Studio's account ends, Happli will make Client Data available for export as described in the Terms and then delete it as described in the Privacy Policy, except where law requires keeping it.

9. Audits

On reasonable written request, no more than once a year, Happli will provide information needed to show compliance with this DPA.

10. International data

Happli stores Client Data with U.S.-based infrastructure providers. Happli currently serves studios in the United States only. Some subprocessors listed under section 5 may process Client Data outside the United States when they provide their services.
