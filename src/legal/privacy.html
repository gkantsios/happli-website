Privacy Policy

Last updated: MERGE_DATE

This policy explains what personal information Happli (Happy Industries, Inc. dba Happli) collects, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. Happli handles two kinds of information:

  • Studio account information: information about studio owners and their team members, such as names, emails and settings. Happli decides how this is used, as described here.
  • Studio client information: information about a studio's clients, such as names, contact details, appointments, form answers and purchases. This belongs to the studio. Happli processes it only on the studio's behalf and instructions, as a service provider (processor) under our Data Processing Addendum. If you're a studio's client, the studio's own privacy policy also applies, and the studio is your first point of contact.

1. What we collect

From studio owners and team members

  • Account and business details: name, email, phone, business name, locations, hours, services and prices, team members and roles, and settings.
  • Billing: your subscription is billed through Stripe. We see the plan, status and the last digits of the card, never full card numbers.
  • Payout setup: to take client payments, you set up a Stripe Connect account directly with Stripe, which collects identity and bank details under its own privacy policy.
  • Usage and logs: sign-ins, an audit log of actions in your account, device and browser details, IP addresses, and error logs.

For studios, about their clients (processed on the studio's behalf)

  • Contact details: name, email and phone.
  • Appointments, services, team member, notes the studio adds, packages, gift cards and purchase history.
  • Forms and waivers clients fill out, including signatures.
  • Message history and text and email opt-out status.
  • Payment records. Card details are entered into Stripe; Happli never sees full card numbers.
  • Client lists a studio imports from another system.
  • How a client found the studio, if the studio uses client source tracking: the general source (for example a Google search, an Instagram post or an ad), campaign tags, the referring website, the page they landed on and, if the studio turns it on, the ad's click code.
  • Website chat conversations and details a visitor chooses to share, if the studio uses the chat widget.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the Service: online booking, the calendar, checkout, reminders, forms, reports and the features a studio turns on.
  • To send messages on a studio's behalf: confirmations and reminders the studio switches on, and campaign messages the studio approves. Win-back ("time to come back") messages are off unless a studio turns them on, and go only to clients who agreed to marketing messages. A thank-you text after a visit, which may include the studio's Google review link, goes only to clients who agreed to marketing texts and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out." Each client gets at most 4 marketing texts a month from a studio.
  • To show a studio how its clients found it, so it can see which ads and posts work. We don't use this for our own advertising and don't share it with advertisers.
  • To send you service emails, such as sign-in links, invitations, billing notices and important updates.
  • To generate AI drafts, summaries and answers. Only the context needed is sent to our AI provider, client prompts use first names only, and under our agreement the provider doesn't use it to train its models.
  • To secure, debug, support and improve the Service, including with aggregated, de-identified data.
  • To comply with law and enforce our terms.

We never sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We never use a studio's client list to market Happli or anyone else.

4. Retention

  • Account and studio client information is kept while the studio's account is active.
  • After an account closes, we delete or de-identify studio data within 90 days, after any export window.
  • Unconfirmed client-import files are deleted after 24 hours. Unrecognized inbound data kept for review is deleted after 30 days.
  • Detailed client source data (campaign tags, ad click codes, the referring website and landing page) is kept only as long as needed and deleted on a schedule we set. The general source stays with the client record until the studio deletes it. Source cookies on studio websites expire after 90 days.
  • Opt-out records are kept so they stay honored. Audit logs and billing records are kept as long as needed for security, legal and tax purposes. Backups roll off on a regular schedule after deletion.

5. Your rights and choices

  • Studio owners can view and update account details in the app and can request access, correction, export or deletion by emailing hello@gohappli.com.
  • Studio clients should contact the studio directly. If you contact us, we'll pass your request to the studio and help it respond.
  • Texts: reply STOP to any text to stop texts from that sender. Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you're a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to request deletion and correction; to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; to limit use of sensitive personal information; and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, and we don't use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics. The categories we collect are listed in section 1, the sources are you, your studio and your use of the Service, and the purposes are in section 2. To make a request, email hello@gohappli.com. We'll verify your identity and respond within 45 days. You can use an authorized agent. For studio client information, we act as the studio's service provider and will refer requests to the studio.

Residents of other states with privacy laws may have similar rights and can make requests the same way. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision or emailing us with "Appeal" in the subject, and we'll respond within the time the law requires.

6. Cookies

We don't use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies. The Happli app uses only essential cookies, to keep you signed in and secure. Studio websites built by Happli, and other studio websites that add Happli's source tracking script, also set a first-party cookie on the studio's own website, hb_src (with a companion cookie, hb_last), that remembers for up to 90 days how a visitor found the studio, so the studio can see which ads and posts bring in bookings. Websites Happli builds include this script by default. It isn't used for advertising or to track you across other websites. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, these cookies aren't set, and only the general source (for example "Instagram") is kept. See our Cookie Notice.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit, encryption at rest for our database and its backups, per-studio data isolation enforced in the database, passwordless sign-in and an append-only audit log. See our Security page. No system is perfectly secure; we'll notify affected studios of a breach as required by law.

8. Children

Happli's service is for businesses and isn't directed to children. Studios may book clients under 18 only with a parent or guardian's consent, and studios are responsible for complying with state law, such as tanning restrictions for minors. Happli doesn't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information directly, email hello@gohappli.com and we'll delete it.

9. Where data is stored

Happli serves studios in the United States only. We store Service data with U.S.-based infrastructure providers. Some of the service providers listed in section 3 may process data in other countries when they provide their services to us.
