Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy is part of our Terms of Service and applies to every studio and team member using Happli. If you break it, we may remove content, pause sending, or suspend or close the account.

Messaging

  • Only text or email people who have a genuine relationship with your studio and have given the consent the law requires. Marketing texts need prior express written consent.
  • Don't import purchased, rented, scraped or shared contact lists.
  • Don't message anyone who has opted out, or try to get around STOP, unsubscribe or sending hours.
  • Don't send content that is false, misleading, harassing, hateful, sexually explicit, or about illegal products. Carrier rules also prohibit SHAFT content (sex, hate, alcohol, firearms, tobacco) and cannabis in many cases.
  • Don't impersonate another person or business or hide who the message is from.

Payments

  • Only take payments for real services and products your studio provides, and follow Stripe's restricted business list.
  • Don't use Happli to process payments for another business.

Data and content

  • Don't upload content you don't have the right to use, or malware.
  • Don't use forms or notes to collect medical records, full card numbers, Social Security numbers or similar sensitive data.

The Service

  • Don't try to access another studio's data or accounts, or share sign-in links.
  • Don't probe, scan or test our systems for vulnerabilities without our written permission. Report security issues to hello@gohappli.com.
  • Don't disrupt, overload, scrape, copy, resell or reverse-engineer the Service, or use it to build a competing product.
  • Don't use the Service to break any law.

Reporting

To report abuse, email hello@gohappli.com.
