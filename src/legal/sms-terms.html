These terms apply to text messages sent through Happli on behalf of the studio you booked with ("the studio"). Happli provides the software; the studio is the sender and is responsible for its messages.

What you'll receive

  • Appointment messages: booking confirmations, reminders and changes for appointments you book. Frequency depends on your bookings, typically one to three messages per appointment.
  • Marketing messages (only if you opt in): offers, updates, and thank-you and review requests after your visit from the studio. Up to 4 marketing messages per month from each studio, thank-you texts included. Consent isn't a condition of any purchase.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP to stop receiving texts. You'll get one confirmation and no further texts unless you opt back in. STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE and STOPALL all work. So does a reply that clearly asks us to stop, such as "stop texting me," "remove me," "don't text me," "wrong number" or "no more." If it isn't clear whether a reply means you want to stop, we pass it to the studio, and it's treated as an opt-out within 10 business days if that's what you meant. While the studio decides, we pause marketing texts to your number; appointment texts, such as reminders, may continue. You can also opt out by telling the studio in any other reasonable way.
  • Reply START to opt back in.
  • Texts may come from a phone number Happli shares among several studios. Replying STOP to a shared number stops texts from every studio that uses it, not only this one.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact the studio directly or email hello@gohappli.com.

Rates and carriers

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers aren't liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We send marketing texts, such as offers, invitations to come back and thank-you texts that ask for a review, only between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. in your local time. For Texas phone numbers, marketing texts start at 9 a.m., or at noon on Sundays, and still end at 8 p.m. We don't send other texts, such as appointment reminders and follow-ups, between 10 p.m. and 8 a.m. in your local time, and a studio may choose a shorter sending window for them. A text due during these quiet hours waits until they end. A few texts you're expecting can go out at any hour: the confirmation of a booking you make yourself, such as the confirmation right after you book online; a text letting you know a team member is on the way to you; and an immediate reply to a text you send us, such as the confirmation after you reply STOP or the answer when you reply HELP. We work out your local time from your phone number's area code, and we also check the time where the studio is: a text goes out only when it's allowed both in your area code's time zone (in every one of them, if the area code covers more than one) and in the studio's time zone. A held text that would no longer be useful, such as a reminder for an appointment that has already started, isn't sent. A thank-you text after your visit may include a link to leave the studio a Google review. It counts as a marketing text: it goes only to people who agreed to get marketing texts, and it ends with "Reply STOP to opt out." For that visit, it takes the place of a separate review request, and a studio asks you for a review at most once every 60 days.
