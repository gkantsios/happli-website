Last updated: October 29, 2026

These terms apply to text messages sent through Happli on behalf of the studio you booked with ("the studio"). Happli provides the software; the studio is the sender and is responsible for its messages.

What you'll receive

  • Appointment messages: booking confirmations, reminders and changes for appointments you book. Frequency depends on your bookings, typically one to three messages per appointment.
  • Marketing messages (only if you opt in): offers and updates from the studio, and thank-you texts and review requests after your visit. Studios choose how often to send marketing texts, and you can reply STOP at any time. Consent isn't a condition of any purchase.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP to stop receiving texts. You'll get one confirmation and no further texts unless you opt back in. STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE and STOPALL all work. You can also opt out by telling the studio in any other reasonable way, and the studio must stop texting you.
  • Reply START to opt back in.
  • Texts may come from a phone number Happli shares among several studios. Replying STOP to a shared number stops texts from every studio that uses it, not only this one.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact the studio directly or email hello@gohappli.com.

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers aren't liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Texts follow the studio's sending hours in the studio's time zone. A thank-you text after your visit is a short thank-you from the studio. It counts as a marketing text: it goes only to people who agreed to get marketing texts, and it ends with "Reply STOP to opt out." For that visit, it takes the place of a separate review request, and a studio asks you for a review at most once every 60 days.

Privacy

Your phone number and opt-in status are used only to send you the studio's messages and are never sold or shared with third parties for their marketing. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties, except with service providers, such as our messaging provider Twilio, that send texts for us.
