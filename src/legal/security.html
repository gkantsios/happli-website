Security

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Happli holds business data on behalf of working businesses, and we treat that as the core responsibility of the product. Here's how the Service is protected today, stated plainly, without badges we haven't earned yet.

Data isolation by design

Every business's data is separated at the database layer using row-level security. Isolation is enforced by the database itself on every query, not just by application code. The application connects with least-privilege database roles: the app role cannot read across tenants and cannot delete records in append-only tables like the audit log.

Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Our database is encrypted at rest by our database provider, and our nightly database backups are encrypted before they're stored. Payment details are handled entirely by Stripe, so full card numbers never touch our systems.

The approval gate as a safety control

A one-off text or email drafted by AI is never sent until you approve it in your Needs You list, and that decision is recorded in the audit trail. If you turn on a follow-up or win-back campaign, messages in that campaign can send after you've reviewed how the campaign works — each live draft is not re-reviewed. The optional website chat replies to visitors on its own from your public business information; it cannot see your client list. Messages a studio switches on in its settings, such as booking confirmations, reminders and thank-you texts, use the studio's own wording, involve no AI, and send automatically. Win-back ("time to come back") messages are off unless the studio turns them on, and go only to clients who agreed to marketing messages.

Auditability

Actions in the Service, such as syncs, drafts, approvals, sends and opt-outs, are recorded in an append-only audit log scoped to your business.

Access and authentication

Sign-in uses secure, expiring email links, so there are no passwords to steal or reuse. Sessions are cookie-based and can be ended by signing out.

Your data and AI

Happli uses Anthropic's commercial API — not the public Claude chat app — to help draft messages, phrase insights, answer questions about your reports, turn a blank form into a draft, and power an optional website chat. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, your studio's data is not used to train their models.

We send only what a feature needs, and we keep each studio's data separate. When AI helps with a message to a client, it sees first names, not phone numbers, emails, or payment details. A one-off text or email is never sent until you approve it. Campaigns you turn on can send follow-ups in your voice after you've reviewed how the campaign works. The optional website chat answers visitors from your public business information only — it cannot see your client list — and phone numbers or emails a visitor types are stripped before the AI sees them.

You can pause a campaign or choose not to use the chat at any time.

Messaging safeguards

Text opt-outs (STOP and similar keywords) are honored immediately and stay in effect until the person opts back in, for example by replying START. Texts follow the studio's sending hours. Links Happli adds to messages go only to fixed destinations, such as a studio's form or review page.

What we haven't done yet, honestly

We're an early-stage company. We don't yet hold formal certifications (such as SOC 2), and we don't yet offer HIPAA business associate agreements. Those are on our roadmap as the Service matures. If your compliance needs are ahead of where we are, tell us; it directly shapes what we build next.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security issue, email hello@gohappli.com with details. We'll acknowledge within 2 business days, and we ask that you give us a reasonable window to fix the issue before public disclosure. We're grateful to people who report responsibly.
